## 💡 Recommendations & Business Impact

| Recommendation | What led to this recommendation | Numbers |
|---|---|---|
| **New state EV policies should copy Goa and Karnataka's approach** | These two states saw the biggest jump in EV share right after their policy and have decent charging infrastructure — much bigger than the national average jump in that time | Goa (Pre-Policy vs Post Policy EV market share): 0.1% → 2.57% (25x), Karnataka: 0.05% → 0.79% (15x) |
| **Haryana's EV policy needs a review** | After the policy, Haryana's EV share barely moved, even though the rest of the country was growing fast at the same time | Haryana: 1.37% → 3.66% (+2.29pp), vs. national +4.62pp in the same window — the only state to fall behind the national trend by more than 2 points |
| **Build more charging stations in Tripura and Assam** | These states already have high EV adoption but very few charging stations — demand is ahead of supply here | Tripura: 18.28% EV share but only 1.3 chargers per 100k people. Assam: 14.30% EV share but only 0.95 chargers per 100k people |
| **Don't assume "more chargers = more EVs" everywhere** | Some states have decent charger numbers but almost no EV adoption | Arunachal Pradesh: 2.83 chargers per 100k people (higher than Tripura), but only 0.25% EV share |
| **3-Wheeler makers should focus on scaling up, not convincing buyers** | Almost 6 out of 10 new 3-wheelers are already electric — buyers are already choosing EVs in this category | 3-Wheeler EV share: 13.36% (FY2017-18) → 58.85% (FY2025-26) |
| **Commercial fleet (buses, taxis, goods vehicles) is a wide-open opportunity for early movers** | Less than 2% of these vehicles are electric so far — this category hasn't picked up yet, unlike 2W/3W | Commercial fleet EV share: 1.85% (FY2025-26), vs. 58.85% for 3-Wheelers |
| **Investors should watch states with a recent policy but no share jump yet** | Some states' policies produced almost no visible lift yet — those may be the ones about to catch up, the way Chandigarh and Goa did | Karnataka (-0.05pp) and Telangana (-0.05pp) show near-zero outperformance so far, despite active EV policies already in place |

## Vehicle Category analysis (FY2025-26)

- Three-Wheelers lead penetration at 58.8% (up from just 13.36% in FY17-18 — the single biggest category-level shift in the dataset), while Two-Wheelers lead volume at 57.66% of all EV units despite only 6.62% penetration within the category.

## EV Policies impact on EV Adoption

- Some of the states responded well to the EV policies (especially the one focused on Subsidy for purchasing the vehicles).

<p align="center">
  <img src="/images/results/Picture6.png" width="600">
</p>

- Goa shows the clearest outperformance — the EV share grew 25x when compared with the EV share during the pre-policy period.

- Goa's policy primarily targeted massive two-wheeler rental fleets, taxis, and ferry systems to green energy, backed by charging stations every 25 km.

## Charging infrastructure (2020–2025)

- While most of the states have a good EV market share, they lack in charging infrastructure.

- Only Karnataka seems to excel in both (EV market share — 7.1% and with 111 EVs per Charger).
